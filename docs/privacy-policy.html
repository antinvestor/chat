Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 23, 2026

Introduction

Antinvestor ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Chat mobile application ("App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your phone number or email address for authentication purposes.
  • Profile Information: Name, profile photo, and status/about information you choose to provide.
  • Messages: Text messages, voice messages, images, videos, and files you send through the App.
  • Contacts: With your permission, we access your device contacts to help you connect with other users.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: App features used, interaction timestamps, and performance data.
  • Log Data: Error logs and diagnostic information to improve app stability.

Permissions We Request

Permission Purpose
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) To record voice messages that you choose to send to other users
Camera To capture photos and videos for sharing in conversations
Contacts To help you find and connect with friends who use the App
Storage To save and access media files you send or receive
Notifications To alert you of new messages and calls
Internet To send and receive messages and sync data

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Send and receive messages on your behalf
  • Enable voice and video calls
  • Sync your data across your devices
  • Send push notifications for new messages
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • Messages and media are stored locally on your device using encrypted SQLite databases
  • Sensitive credentials are stored using platform-secure storage (Keychain/Keystore)

Server Storage

  • Messages are transmitted through our secure servers for delivery
  • We use TLS encryption for all network communications
  • End-to-end encryption (E2EE) is available for enhanced privacy

Data Retention

  • Messages are stored on our servers only until delivered
  • You can delete your messages and account data at any time
  • Deleted data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days

Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information.

We may share information only in these circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you explicitly agree to share information
  • With other users: Messages and profile information you choose to share
  • Service providers: Third-party services that help us operate the App (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics)
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that collect information:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: For push notifications (Google's Privacy Policy applies)
  • Sentry: For error tracking and crash reporting (anonymized data only)

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data
  • Portability: Export your data in a standard format
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions at any time through device settings

Managing Permissions

You can manage app permissions in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Chat → Permissions
  • iOS: Settings → Chat → Permissions

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending a notification for significant changes
